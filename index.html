SpellCircuit turns a weekly school spelling list into short, varied practice sessions.

Scan or enter school words, review the exact spellings, and practise with Spelling Test, Quick Practice, Listen & Spell, Word Builder, Spot the Mistake, Look–Cover–Spell–Check and Word Search. School results and app practice stay separate.

Get help

A parent or guardian can email josephmunford@icloud.com. Include the app version/build, iPhone model, iOS version, activity and steps that led to the problem. Please do not send a child’s name, school, private worksheet, passwords or account codes. Use made-up words in screenshots where possible.

Start a spelling week

  1. Choose Scan Word List or Enter Words Manually.
  2. Review each spelling and its order. Suggestions never replace a confirmed school spelling automatically.
  3. Confirm the list, save it, then choose a practice activity.

Starting a new week archives the previous list and retains its saved results. Samples and Practice Preview do not add school progress.

Keep your progress

Lists, practice, school results and settings are stored on your iPhone. Keep the app installed when updating; deleting it can remove local data. Manage Saved Data offers an export. A normal iPhone backup may include app data. Data is not automatically shared between family members’ phones.

Words, voices and controls

Medium is the starting difficulty for quizzes and Word Search. Your saved choices take priority. Graded spelling uses letter buttons without dictation, autocorrect or paste. Press and hold a letter for accented alternatives. In Word Builder, Edit Letters provides large controls to move or remove letters from long words. Sound, voice and haptics choices are in Settings.

Offline and cloud help

Manual entry, existing lists, local games and saved validated questions remain available offline. Optional Apple Private Cloud Compute assistance needs a compatible device, Apple Intelligence availability and internet access. A parent should approve cloud use. Only confirmed spelling words or the cropped worksheet are sent; crop out names and unrelated details first. Cloud unavailability does not erase your list.

Study reminders

Optional local reminders cover Monday–Thursday practice and Friday’s spelling test. There are no weekend reminders. Permission and times can be managed in Settings. Reminders apply to the active school week rather than repeating indefinitely.

Family beta feedback

If you are invited through TestFlight, use Send Beta Feedback. Tell us about wrong choices or grading, pronunciation, long words, unclear instructions, unresponsive buttons, layout, lost progress, crashes or freezes. Parents should review screenshots before sending them. Apple manages TestFlight feedback and crash reporting.

SpellCircuit has no advertising, third-party tracking, subscriptions or in-app purchases. Read the privacy policy.
