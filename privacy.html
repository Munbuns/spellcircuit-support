SpellCircuit Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026 · Developer: Joseph Munford

SpellCircuit is a spelling-study app for families. No app account, real name, birth date, school or email address is required to study. An optional nickname stays in local app data.

Study data stays on your iPhone

Word lists and their order, archived weeks, answers, practice scores, manually entered school results, puzzle progress, achievements, unfinished answer drafts and preferences are stored locally. SpellCircuit does not operate a server that receives your profile or learning history. The app does not automatically sync or copy this data to other family members’ devices.

Worksheet images and scanning

Camera access is used only when you choose scanning. You can choose an individual photo with Apple’s photo picker. On-device Vision recognition does not send that image to the developer. SpellCircuit does not add worksheet scans to your Photos library. Apple’s scanning components can leave temporary scan images in the app’s local cache; these may remain until iOS removes them and may be included in device backups. SpellCircuit does not upload these cached files to its developer.

Optional Apple Private Cloud Compute

Cloud assistance is off unless permitted in Settings. When allowed, confirmed spelling words may be sent to Apple to prepare plausible spelling-question choices. If you choose cloud interpretation while scanning, the cropped worksheet image is sent to Apple. Crop out names, schools, faces and unrelated information before using this option. No nickname, score history or full profile is included in these requests. Manual entry, on-device scanning and local study remain available without cloud assistance.

Requests use Apple’s Foundation Models framework and Private Cloud Compute, not a third-party AI provider. Apple describes PCC as using request data to fulfil the request without retaining it afterwards or making it accessible to Apple. See Apple’s PCC privacy and security description. Device, account, region, availability and usage limits may affect this optional service. Validated question results can be cached on your device. Turning cloud assistance off stops new automatic cloud preparation.

Voice, haptics and notifications

Speech uses Apple’s installed system voices. SpellCircuit does not record your voice or request microphone access. Graded spelling uses custom letter controls, not dictation. Optional local study notifications are scheduled on your iPhone; there is no developer push-notification service. Saved sound, voice and haptic preferences remain on the device.

No advertising or tracking

SpellCircuit contains no ads, advertising identifier access, third-party analytics or tracking SDK. It does not sell data or use schoolwork for advertising. There are no purchases or subscriptions in this beta.

Backups, exports and deletion

Apple’s normal device/iCloud backup can include app data according to your iPhone settings. If you choose to export study data, the file is saved to the destination you select and can contain words, answers and results; share it carefully. App data management can remove local history after confirmation. Deleting the app can remove its local study data. The developer cannot recover data that was never sent to them.

TestFlight and voluntary support

Apple manages TestFlight distribution, beta feedback and crash reporting under its own terms. Feedback you choose to send may include device/app information, screenshots and crash details, and may be available to the developer. A parent should review feedback and remove children’s identifiers or private schoolwork before sending it. If you email support, the developer receives your email address and the information you include, uses it to respond and investigate, and does not use it for advertising. Please contact support to request removal of support correspondence; Apple controls its own TestFlight records.

Children and parents

A parent or guardian should supervise optional cloud use, exports and support/feedback submissions. Please do not provide a child’s personal information. Spelling words entered locally do not need to identify the child. If personal information is accidentally sent directly to support, contact the developer to request deletion.

This website

This static support website is hosted by GitHub Pages and has no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts added by the developer. GitHub may process normal request information to operate and secure the site under GitHub’s privacy statement. This website does not receive the app’s local spelling data.

Contact and updates

A parent or guardian can contact josephmunford@icloud.com about privacy. Changes to this policy will be dated on this page and will reflect the app’s actual behaviour.
